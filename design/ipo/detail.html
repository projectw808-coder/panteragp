About this offering

Helios Grid Series B funds three metered solar installations already under contract. Returns are paid from output measured at the meter rather than from a forecast, and the term runs 180 days from the day the book closes.

Allocation is first come, first served. A subscription that would take the book past its target is refused with the amount still available, rather than scaled back — there is no pro-rata.
